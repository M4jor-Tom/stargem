## Evidence and disposition policy

Every local assertion refers to the inspected baseline, not a live Star Conflict client. **Contradiction** means explicit opposing documentary claims under a shared identity/universal-fidelity interpretation; it does not prove the independent Stargem design wrong. **Intentional simplification** means an explicit narrower local commitment, not proof the author considered every external exception. **Scope gap** means absent representation, often explicitly excluded, not a defect. **Historical or uncertain** keeps dated changes, source conflicts and undecided identity visible. Actual historical change is only asserted with dated evidence. Internal root causes belong only in ISSUE items.

Every linked topic/source ID resolves to its exact URL, publisher/type, visible publication/edit date or unknown, revision where available, and short supporting evidence. **All were retrieved 2026-09-16.** Wiki edit dates are not patch-validity guarantees, and shared templates can be newer than article footers. No game-client behavior was experimentally verified. High documentary confidence does not mean high current-balance confidence. Precise local adoption always waits for QUESTION-001/002; closure can legitimately retain a simplification or exclusion.
